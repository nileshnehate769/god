Chapter 8 Rescue
Lin Tu was hesitating whether to go up and save the woman when the beautiful stall owner suddenly pulled Lin Tu away.
"You clearly know how to save people, what are you standing there watching for? You'll only regret it when people die. Hurry up and go save them."
The beautiful woman roared angrily.
Lin Tu was completely stunned by the shout. What was going on?
Now they dare to yell at me.
Lin Tu stared back incredulously.
"Alright, alright, stop making a fuss. Let's get this person back first."
Lin Tu glanced at the woman lying on the ground, suppressing her anger. Forget it, she didn't want to talk to him about it.
Lin Tu took out a pill from her bosom and handed it to the woman, urging her to swallow it quickly.
The woman lying on the ground hesitated upon seeing what Lin Tu was holding. Could this thing really be eaten? Would she die if she ate it?
The woman hesitated and kept backing away, but Lin Tu grabbed her.
"Let me be very clear: if you want to survive, you have to eat this."
Lin Tu took out the pills again and handed them to the woman. The woman then forced herself to swallow them in one gulp, suppressing her discomfort.
After the medicine entered her stomach, the woman felt much better.
"Thank you."
The woman on the ground slowly stood up, brushed the dust off her body, and said...
Although I don't know what Lin Tu gave me to eat, it did save my life in any case.
Lin Tu nodded slightly, looking the woman up and down.
The woman looked quite young and pretty; she probably wasn't from an ordinary family.
It's just that I probably don't eat breakfast often because of work.
If you keep damaging your body like this, it definitely won't work.
"Remember to eat breakfast before going out next time, otherwise you won't be lying here next time, you don't know who will take you away next time."
Lin Tu kindly reminded the woman.
The woman turned her head away angrily.
"What's it to you? You only saved me once, does that give you the right to tell me what to do with you?"
Lin Tu was about to leave when she heard the woman's voice and was completely bewildered.
Lin Tu had no malicious intent whatsoever.
"Okay, it seems I was just being presumptuous. Be careful on your way back."
Considering the woman was in a bad mood, Lin Tu decided not to argue with him any further.
"This is my business card. If you need my help in the future, you can call this number to find me, but don't call me unless it's necessary."
Lin Tu took the business card and looked at it.
The words "President Jiang Ruolin" are written on it.
No wonder Lin Tu said that this woman's temperament was different from others; she was actually a CEO. He really underestimated her.
"Don't worry, I definitely won't look for you. But if you can't find me, then I won't know. Let me tell you, I'll be here from now on. If you want to find me, come here."
Lin Tu waved to Jiang Ruolin's departing figure. Lin Tu had a premonition that he would definitely come back again; it was only a matter of time.
Wang Xiaoran watched in stunned silence. She glanced at Lin Tu and realized that Lin Tu was indeed quite capable. Moreover, it had healed the person it had saved. It seemed that Lin Tu was truly extraordinary.
