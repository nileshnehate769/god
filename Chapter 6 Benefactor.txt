Chapter 6 Benefactor
The old man was still quite lucid; he knew that he had fallen down on his own, not that Lin Tu had pushed him.
Hearing this, Lin Tu felt very relieved. Fortunately, she hadn't been bitten in return, otherwise she would have been furious.
"It's alright, old man. You did collapse just now, but I managed to save you. Next time you go out, be sure to take your medicine with you. You really can't live without it; it could take your life at any time!"
Lin Tu looked at the old man with a serious expression and warned him that he had heart disease and needed to carry his medication with him.
Otherwise, he could have disappeared from this world in the blink of an eye, the old man said, his eyes filled with tears.
I am very grateful to Lin Tu. If it weren't for Lin Tu today, I might already be dead.
Wang Xiaoran also rushed over in a panic and looked at Lin Tu, asking her a question.
"How is it? Is it all better now?"
Wang Xiaoran was afraid that some inexplicable trouble would come her way as more and more people gathered around her stall.
Lin Tu comforted Wang Xiaoran.
"It's alright, don't worry. This old man's illness has been cured. Now stand up and let's see how he is."
Lin Tu supported the old man with one hand, while using the other hand to massage the old man's heart.
After the old man slowly stood up, he felt a very different feeling in his body.
The two people looked extremely shocked, wondering what had happened in those few short minutes.
Why doesn't my heart experience that excruciating pain?
And his headache was gone. The old man took a few big steps. Good heavens, he was walking so briskly. What happened to this young man?
What did I just do to myself?
The old man grabbed Lin Tu's hand with a slight excitement.
"Young man, what exactly did you do to me? I was on the verge of death, but after you treated me like that, I feel refreshed and energetic."
Lin Tu could also tell that the old man was noticeably different from before, and he spoke with a particularly strong voice.
This made Lin Tu very embarrassed.
Passersby also cast surprised glances at Lin Tu, wondering what she had just done to the old man.
Many passersby also took out their mobile phones.
They kept taking pictures of Lin Tu. Hehe, but that's nothing. Lin Tu asked them to be quiet for a moment.
"Let me tell you about it today," Lin Tu said slowly.
"It's nothing, it's nothing. I just gave you a pill from our family's heirloom, and you got better. But you have to be careful when you go out in the future, and don't go out alone again."
Lin Tu didn't take the matter to heart; instead, her words were full of concern for the old man.
This stirred up a lot of excitement in the old man.
Such a relationship is extremely rare, and this young man not only saved him but also cared for him so much.
Just then, the old man took a card out of his pocket and handed it to Lin Tu.
This card has unlimited funds; I hope Lin Tu will accept it.
"Young man, this is a small token of my appreciation. Please don't look down on it, you absolutely must accept it. It's not much money here, really."
The old man nodded slightly and said in a trembling voice that although there was an unlimited amount of money here.
But these things are nothing compared to the trust of the elderly.
The old man was willing to give all his money to Lin Tu just to survive.
Lin Tu frowned slightly upon seeing this. Lin Tu didn't want this. He wasn't trying to get the money from him, so what was he doing?
Did Lin Tu only save people for money?
Lin Tu was worried that others might say something about her, so she immediately refused and handed the card back to the old man.
"No, no, no, you're too kind. The reason I saved you today was purely by chance, not what you're thinking. Please accept this; it's really not necessary."
Lin Tu blushed as the old man praised her, but he insisted on giving her the card.
If Lin Tu doesn't charge money today, the old man has decided to stay here and not leave.
"You should accept this. By the way, this is my business card. If you need anything in the future, you can just call this number to find me."
The old man took out a business card from his person for the first time, and Lin Tu looked down at it.
This is a gold card with only a name and a string of numbers on it.
Nothing else was written, nor was it stated what the old man did for a living.
Lin Tu accepted the business card for the time being, but she really didn't intend to accept the card.
There is ample time.
Who knows, maybe in the future neither of us will need the other?
"Let them forget about it, it's really just a small favor. You should go home now, or I can call your family and have them come pick you up."
Lin Tu was really going all out to be a good person, and the old man quickly smiled and waved his hand.
At this moment, the old man was overjoyed, as he had thought he was about to die.
Unexpectedly, the young man ate something that made him look 30 years younger.
Young people are really something! The old man stretched out his arms and legs in a long, lazy yawn. Good heavens!
This feeling is so wonderful; it really brings back that youthful feeling.
"Young man, do you sell that kind of miracle drug that your family makes? I don't know what you gave me, but I'm willing to spend all my money to buy that drug."
The old man suddenly brought up this matter, and Lin Tu gave an awkward smile, saying that there was no such thing as a miracle drug.
Those things were all fabricated by Lin Tu.
However, Lin Tu could never directly tell the old man that he had made it up himself.
Wouldn't the old man still be filled with doubt?
Lin Tu had no choice but to come up with another reason.
"We don't sell our medicine to anyone, old man. If you have any problems in the future, you can come to me here, because I might set up a stall here."
Just now, this thought popped into Lin Tu's mind: this place is crowded and lively.
In addition, there are often elderly people here, so this is a good opportunity for me to set up a stall here.
I saw Lin Tu's idea, but now there's nothing to sell at the stall.
If I were to set up a medical stall here, people would probably think I'm crazy or a charlatan.
