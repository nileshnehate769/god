Chapter 7 The Conman
It's alright though.
Let them make up whatever they want; I just need to do what I want to do.
Upon hearing about this unusual action, the old man nodded emphatically, saying, "I'll come here to find Lin Tu from now on."
With a chuckle, the old man patted Lin Tu on the shoulder and left, quickly disappearing into the crowd.
Seeing the old man leave with a smile, Wang Xiaoran quickly walked to Lin Tu's side.
"What did you eat with that old man? He left so happily, you didn't give him any poison, did you?"
Lin Tu turned his head and saw Wang Xiaoran looking completely innocent, her big eyes staring at him.
I'm really baffled by Lin Tu's train of thought; where does she get this kind of logic from?
How could Lin Tu possibly have poison? And why would she feed that old man poison in public?
Haha, that's hilarious!
Even novels can't depict such a brilliant, godlike scene.
The key point is that she's such a beautiful woman, and Lin Tu felt she simply couldn't accept what she said.
Lin Tu immediately shook her head and advised him to go get his brain checked first, saying that if there was anything wrong with his brain, a visit to the hospital would solve the problem.
However, Lin Tu only thought about sending the person to see a doctor for a moment.
How could Lin Tu possibly tell a beautiful woman to go to the hospital to have her brain checked?
I would say this was a way of saving this beautiful woman; if it weren't for Lin Tu, everyone else would have blamed her for it.
"Alright, we're destined to meet. Hello, my name is Lin Tu."
Lin Tu extended her right hand and greeted Wang Xiaoran.
"Hello, my name is Wang Xiaoran. What you said is true. If it weren't for you just now, I think I would have been dead. Luckily you were there."
Wang Xiaoran nodded slightly and said.
Finally, Wang Xiaoran returned to her stall, exhausted, to tidy up the items.
They started setting up a real stall.
Lin Tu noticed that Wang Xiaoran was selling a very diverse range of items.
It's mostly stuff that girls like, plus some celebrity posters and autographed photos.
There are even cosmetics here; this place is practically a storage room.
Lin Tu happened to have some questions about setting up a stall and wanted to consult him. She wondered if he could help her.
"Now that we've gotten to know each other, I'd like to ask you about some things. I wonder if I can get any information from you?"
Lin Tu immediately walked to Wang Xiaoran's side. Although they had only just met, Lin Tu acted as if they were complete strangers.
"What do you want? I asked you for my contact information, but I don't have it and I won't give it to you. Why don't you buy a few things from me?"
Wang Xiaoran didn't care at all that Lin Tu had already entered.
Instead, he busied himself tidying up the things on his stall.
"You guys seem to be making a lot of money setting up stalls here. Could you tell me how to set up a stall? I want to rent a stall here too..."
Lin Tu simply and clearly stated her purpose for coming.
As a result, Wang Xiaoran completely ignored him. Who would talk to him about something like stealing business?
Wang Xiaoran rolled her eyes and advised her to set up her stall somewhere else, saying that the market for food, goods, and entertainment here was already saturated.
Even if Lin Tu sets up a stall here, he definitely won't make much money.
"Young man, I advise you to look elsewhere. I'm telling you, there are other places with similar food streets, but this place is really not good; it's already saturated."
Wang Xiaoran spoke softly, her words clearly conveying her concern for Lin Tu.
Fearing that Lin Tu would lose money on his investments, but having nothing else to invest in, Lin Tu bought a small table and sat down.
Anyone who's sick can come to see me. Besides, it's also a way to kill time; it's actually quite boring all day long.
Setting up a stall here allows you to see different beautiful women and kill time, and Wang Xiaoran's stall is right next to you.
How could Lin Tu pass up such a good opportunity?
"Haha, beautiful lady, I know what you're thinking. Right now, you're probably thinking about how to avoid competing with me for business, right? I can tell you very clearly that not only will I not compete with you for business, I will also help you bring in some business. I'm going to set up a stall here to treat patients."
Lin Tu proudly and calmly announced her plan to set up a stall.
Wang Xiaoran still gave Lin Tu a look of utter disdain.
It was only then that Wang Xiaoran noticed how stubborn Lin Tu looked.
He was such a handsome man, but nowadays, do young men already look like this?
Now they're setting up a medical stall here? Who would come to a street stall for medical treatment?
This is a joke bigger than an international joke, isn't it? Or are they treating me like a fool? Wang Xiaoran scoffed coldly.
"Your acting is so youthful and you're so handsome, but I never expected that you'd already be mentally impaired at such a young age."
"No, I'm serious. I really mean to set up a stall here. Godfather, I have all these clothes but nowhere to use them. I'm so bored, so I came up with this idea. What do you think?"
Lin Tu said somewhat idly, and Wang Xiaoran then remembered the scene of Lin Tu so boldly saving people just now. To be honest, if it were really possible, that would be fine, after all, he has such skills.
However, this business isn't necessarily guaranteed to be very successful.
Just then, a woman dressed plainly but with an excellent demeanor passed by Lin Tu.
As the woman passed by Lin Tu, she looked him up and down twice.
Without saying a word, he turned and walked to another place.
The woman was dressed very plainly, but her identity was quite remarkable.
"Ouch, it hurts."
Suddenly, the woman screamed, clutching her heart, and quickly climbed to the ground, her face contorted in extreme pain.
Upon seeing this, Lin Tu immediately went to the woman's side and asked her very considerately what had happened.
"What's wrong? Is it a stomachache or a heartache?"
Lin Tu glanced at the woman, her actions making her somewhat confused.
Lin Tu heard the woman's shout as she passed by very clearly.
"It must be a stomach problem. I was in a rush to go to work this morning, so I didn't have time to take my medicine."
The woman closed her eyes slightly, her eyelashes trembling uncontrollably, revealing that she was in great pain and desperately needed someone to rescue her.
