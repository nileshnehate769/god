Chapter 10 Business is Good
Sigh, but half of the people are men. Why are men the ones buying things?
Lin Tu could guess what these men were thinking even with her bare hands.
Lin Tu, who was also a man, sighed deeply.
"Look, all these men are coming for you, haven't you noticed?"
Lin Tu gave Wang Xiaoran a special reminder, but Wang Xiaoran didn't say anything, just rolled her eyes and ignored Lin Tu, continuing with her work.
It wasn't until late at night that Wang Xiaoran slowly sat down.
"Alright, you've been looking around for so long, have you come to a conclusion yet? Do you really need to see a doctor here?"
Wang Xiaoran was afraid that Lin Tu might not be thinking clearly, so she asked her again.
That's right, Lin Tu has already thought it through. No matter who asks this question now, Lin Tu will still give the same answer.
The money has already been paid, otherwise, hahaha, and this is a function provided by the system, Lin Tu feels it would be a waste not to use it.
It's because others didn't think of this point or didn't understand it.
So this is what friends are like, Lin Tu answered Wang Xiaoran with absolute certainty.
Wang Xiaoran was speechless; she had never met such a bizarre person before.
Wang Xiaoran had no way to persuade Lin Tu any further, so she could only wish Lin Tu a prosperous business.
I hope Lin Tu's business will be just as successful as his.
Lin Tu said something else.
"Wang Xiaoran, haven't you noticed? All the people buying food from your stall are men. They're not exactly friendly. How can you, a girl, be safe here?"
"I'll be by your side from now on, and I'll be able to help you in some ways."
Wang Xiaoran never took those men seriously. She was alone the whole time and didn't feel like anything was wrong.
He's been here all these years by himself. Hmph, with their petty schemes, what do they think they can do to me?
If I were afraid of them, I wouldn't be here now.
However, there is something I need to tell Lin Tu: there is indeed a group of people who come here to cause trouble from time to time.
It's similar to the kind of thing that involves collecting protection money; sometimes they don't even pay for food.
The people in this area are helpless against them; their power is simply too great.
So there's nothing you can do but endure whatever comes your way.
After thinking about this, a faint sadness appeared on Wang Xiaoran's face, and she fell silent.
Lin Tu didn't know what had happened. Lin Tu glanced at Wang Xiaoran and said.
"What's wrong? Is something really wrong? If so, just say it. You know, it's a grown man like me here..."
"If you don't tell me, you'll miss your chance."
After thinking about it for a while, Wang Xiaoran finally told him the truth; it was better for him to know sooner rather than later.
Then Wang Xiaoran spoke up.
“There’s a group of thugs on this street who often come here to collect protection money, or they eat without paying, even though they make a lot of money.”
But they also want a lot.
Wang Xiaoran fidgeted with the hem of her clothes unhappily.
There's actually such a thing. Look, Lin Tu is right to say that she's bad at the game.
Lin Tu's presence here was useful. Let Lin Tu handle this. After taking Wang Xiaoran home, Lin Tu also went home to rest.
