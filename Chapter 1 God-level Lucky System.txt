Chapter 1: God-Tier Luck System
Baicheng.
A shared bicycle sped by.
"Excuse me!"
"Please move aside!"
Lin Tu was indeed in a hurry.
He had just graduated from university, and a rich second-generation classmate was inviting everyone to a meal. How could he miss the chance for a free meal?
Just as he turned the corner.
Beep! Beep! Beep!!!
Hearing the BMW’s horn, Lin Tu could only watch with wide eyes as the accident unfolded before him.
Just then.
A system, something only seen in novels, appeared.
【Ding】
【Host detected in danger, God-Tier Luck System activated】
【Would you like to use one of your three God-Tier Luck chances?】
Damn it!
I'm about to be run over, and you're asking me such a profound question?
"Yes!"
As soon as Lin Tu made his choice, he heard a violent collision.
His shared bicycle was sent flying over ten meters.
Lin Tu sat on the road, quickly feeling himself to see if any parts were missing. He soon found that apart from his pants being dirty, he was perfectly fine.
The BMW driver also got out to check.
Seeing the severely damaged front of his car, and then seeing Lin Tu unharmed, he calmly pulled out a pack of Zhonghua cigarettes, took a puff, and said, "Call your parents."
"Uncle, I'm fine, no need to call my parents. They... are still working in the factory," Lin Tu sighed, feeling that there were so many good people in the world, caring about others even when their car was so damaged.
But the next moment, Lin Tu started cursing.
The BMW driver spewed vulgarities, cursing, "Damn it, I'm telling you to call your parents to discuss compensation!"
"But my family has no money, Uncle. How about this, I just graduated from university, I can work for you to pay off the debt." Lin Tu panicked. He shouldn't have been greedy for that meal. If he hadn't been greedy, this wouldn't have happened. His parents worked in factories, it was already hard enough for them to support his education, and now he had to pay for car damages.
"Get lost! Who wants to hire a useless university student like you? Call them now, or I'll call the police."
"My family really has no money!" Lin Tu grew anxious.
Just then.
A Mercedes-Benz stopped nearby.
The car door opened, and a beautiful figure stepped out.
With long flowing hair and an elegant demeanor, especially those fair, long legs, it was none other than the campus goddess, Zhang Xueyao, also Lin Tu's classmate.
Unlike other girls who relied on their looks and family wealth, Zhang Xueyao was kind-hearted and never looked down on anyone, which made her the most popular person in school.
Seeing Zhang Xueyao appear, Lin Tu inexplicably wanted to bury his head.
"Lin Tu? What happened?" Zhang Xueyao walked up to Lin Tu. Seeing him sitting on the ground and the damaged front of the BMW, she immediately understood something.
"You're his girlfriend, right? Oh, not bad, so pretty. Let me tell you, he's the one who damaged my car like this. The repair cost will be at least two or three hundred thousand."
The BMW driver looked at Zhang Xueyao lecherously, already planning his next lines. If she had no money, he would ask her to compensate by going to a hotel with him.
"Alright, I'll pay."
Zhang Xueyao pulled out a card from her bag without a second thought.
But how could Lin Tu let the goddess pay for him? He quickly stopped her, saying, "No, no, I'll call my parents."
"But your parents..."
Zhang Xueyao wanted to say, 'Do your parents have money?'
After all, everyone in school knew that Lin Tu had been called out multiple times by the school for overdue tuition and was almost expelled. If he couldn't even afford ten thousand in tuition, how could he come up with two hundred thousand?
But saying it directly would hurt Lin Tu's pride.
At this moment, Lin Tu had already dialed his mother's phone.
"Mom, I hit someone's car." Lin Tu's voice trembled, fearing his mother would be shocked.
But what he didn't expect was.
His mother, who usually only complained about being poor over the phone, just calmly said "Oh" after hearing it. Lin Tu vaguely heard the sound of mahjong.
But wasn't his mother working in the factory? Had she become so poor that she was working part-time at a mahjong parlor?
"Tell your mother that you hit a BMW worth two million!" The driver added at the side.
"Mom, he wants me to pay two hundred thousand."
"Oh."
Hearing his mother's indifferent voice again, Lin Tu wondered if his mother was preparing for a second child.
"I've already told Uncle Qi to go handle it. Just wait, I'm playing mahjong. If anything, find your dad."
"Mom?"
Lin Tu was bewildered.
Wasn't Uncle Qi the one who always came to their house to collect debts? And if he couldn't collect, he would speak very nicely and say he'd come again next time.
It seemed his mother was unreliable.
Calling his father was useless too; the family's financial power was with his mother.
Seeing the BMW driver looking at him with amusement, Lin Tu knew he must have heard the phone conversation. But even if he got beaten up or dragged to the police station, he couldn't lose face in front of Zhang Xueyao.
"Please wait a moment, my mother has already called someone to handle it..." Lin Tu said without much confidence, then turned to Zhang Xueyao and said, "Senior, you go to the hotel to eat first, I'll go over after I deal with this."
Who knew Zhang Xueyao would shake her head as if she saw through him.
"System, System, are you there? Quickly think of a way! Is there a way for me not to lose face in front of Zhang Xueyao and also solve this matter?"
【Of course there is】
【Would you like to consume today's second luck chance?】
"That's a no-brainer!"
Lin Tu activated his second luck chance.
【Ding-dong】
【God-Tier Luck activated successfully. Duration: Three hours】
Boom~
The extremely pleasant sound of a V12 engine roared beside them, drawing everyone's attention.
Immediately, they saw a Rolls-Royce Phantom stop behind the BMW.
Then, a man in a suit, in his forties, stepped out.
"Uncle Qi?"
Lin Tu stammered.
"You... know him?"
The BMW driver's speech also became a bit slurred when he heard Lin Tu's words. After all, someone who could drive a Rolls-Royce was definitely not an ordinary person, let alone the noble among Rolls-Royces.
Lin Tu really wanted to say, 'He's my family's creditor.'
But at this moment, Uncle Qi had already walked over, politely handed him his business card, and then said, "Hello, I am the butler of the Lin family, responsible for handling Young Master's accident."
"Young Master?"
The BMW driver's face instantly changed.
He took the card and almost collapsed to the ground in fright.
Lin Group Enterprise!
That super-rich family with assets rumored to be over a trillion!
What does a trillion mean?
If a person won the lottery every day, it would still take more than eight thousand years. If you started buying lottery tickets in the Western Zhou Dynasty, you still wouldn't have this much wealth!
And this unassuming young man in front of him was actually the Young Master?
The BMW driver just wanted to say, 'Is this a damn play?!'
"If you don't believe me, you can check. Our CEO and the Young Master are like two peas in a pod." Uncle Qi said with a smile, "After we handle the compensation, we'll deal with the matter of you almost injuring my Young Master. This is a crosswalk, and looking at your brake marks, you were speeding."
The BMW driver, of course, didn't believe it.
He quickly took out his phone and searched on Baidu. The picture of the Lin Group's CEO quickly appeared. With just one glance, he believed it. Not just two peas in a pod, if it weren't for the slightly different aura, it would be the person himself!
"Young Master, I was wrong! Please treat me like a fart and let me go!"
The BMW driver lost his previous arrogance and immediately knelt down.
