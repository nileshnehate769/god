Chapter 2: He Really Is the Young Master
The car accident was quickly resolved.
The BMW driver paid three thousand.
Uncle Qi drove back.
At this moment, Lin Tu was sitting in the Mercedes, next to Zhang Xueyao, who exuded a faint, pure maidenly fragrance.
"You said all this just now was fake?" Zhang Xueyao was a bit incredulous; after all, she had been terrified just now, and Lin Tu was actually the heir to trillions in assets.
But listening to Lin Tu explain that the person was merely someone who owed their family money and was just putting on a show, Zhang Xueyao felt a bit confused.
"If I really had trillions in assets, how could I not even afford tuition?" Seeing Zhang Xueyao's disbelief, Lin Tu explained with a bitter expression.
Zhang Xueyao nodded.
But her eyes still showed a strange look.
Soon, they arrived at the hotel.
Upon seeing Zhang Xueyao's family car, a well-groomed young man in designer clothes quickly came over, "Zhang Xueyao, you're finally here! I've been waiting for you for over an hour."
The young man was Lu Jiawei, whose family owned three factories; he was the rich second generation in their class, and he was the one hosting the dinner at the hotel this time.
The Mercedes' door opened.
However, Lu Jiawei discovered that Lin Poor was actually sitting with Zhang Xueyao, and his face immediately turned cold.
"Lin Poor, you're quite something," Lu Jiawei said with a cold voice, a hint of threat in his tone. Everyone in university knew that Zhang Xueyao was his designated girlfriend and would consciously keep their distance. What did it mean for Lin Poor to be sitting in Zhang Xueyao's car now?
"I just hitched a ride, that's all," Lin Tu felt a bit disgusted with Lu Jiawei, who spoke as if Zhang Xueyao needed his approval to associate with anyone.
"It better be that way, or I'll find someone to kill you."
After Lu Jiawei finished speaking into Lin Tu's ear, he diligently went to greet Zhang Xueyao.
Some female classmates behind them naturally looked displeased.
So they vented their anger on Lin Tu.
"Lin Poor, someone like you, do you even have the nerve to come to a class reunion? Aren't you afraid of losing face?"
"Juanjuan, don't say that. It's a rare chance to get a free meal; how could he miss such an opportunity?"
"If I were him, I'd have jumped into a river and died already, to avoid affecting the city's appearance."
The words became more and more excessive.
Just then.
【Ding Dong】
【Received Side Quest: Slap the Female Classmates】
【Reward: Unlock Sign-in】
The God-tier Luck System, which had saved Lin Tu twice, appeared.
Hearing the quest details, Lin Tu's face turned bitter. How was he supposed to complete this quest of slapping the female classmates?
Was he supposed to give each of them two slaps?
"System, how exactly do I do this?"
"If you don't tell me, I'll just have to give them two slaps each, and don't you dare go back on your word then."
"Then I'm starting."
Lin Tu was prepared to be beaten to a pulp by the male classmates later.
Fortunately, the System appeared in time to explain: 【Slapping means making them regret having just humiliated you.】
"So, if I lie on the ground and play dead, that counts too?" Lin Tu asked very seriously.
Who knew this System had no sense of humor at all, and just replied: 【If the host wishes to uninstall, the System will deactivate in five minutes.】
"I'll accept, I'll accept, alright?"
Lin Tu was helpless.
He still had one last God-tier Luck, and he was trying to save it.
Now it seemed, this System was determined for him to use it all up and activate this bizarre sign-in feature.
Could it be that signing in would grant three more God-tier Lucks?
That was ridiculous.
However, Lin Tu didn't use it immediately; maybe he could find a better opportunity later.
But Lin Tu quickly paid the price for this decision.
Lu Jiawei, like Zhang Xueyao's personal boyfriend, escorted her inside, and the other classmates followed suit.
Lin Tu was the last to enter.
But when he tried to go in, he was stopped by the security guard.
"Sir, please show your invitation. We cannot let you in without an invitation."
"Invitation?"
Lin Tu pointed at the classmates walking ahead, "Then why don't they need one?"
"Because they are all Young Master Lu's classmates."
The security guard looked at Lin Tu with disdain, "If you don't have an invitation, then please leave. Of course, if you crawl in like a dog, we can pretend not to see it."
Lin Tu immediately understood.
This was Lu Jiawei's deliberate arrangement to humiliate him, just because he had been sitting in Zhang Xueyao's car.
Lin Tu naturally couldn't just leave like this.
Not only did he want to go in, but he also wanted to chat with Zhang Xueyao right in front of Lu Jiawei, add her contact information, and even ask her out!
But if he activated God-tier Luck to get in now, what about the'slap the face' quest?
Just then.
Lin Tu suddenly remembered the introduction for the second God-tier Luck activation: God-tier Luck successfully activated, duration: three hours.
Even if there was a slight delay due to the accident, at most, a little over an hour had passed by now.
Meaning.
The God-tier Luck effect was still active; he was still the young master of a trillion-dollar conglomerate.
He'd know whether it was true or not by trying it.
So Lin Tu gave the security guard a thumbs up, then took out his phone to make a call. The security guard merely sneered at this.
"May I ask if you have an appointment?"
Lin Tu was, of course, calling the Lin Corporation, and then a sweet secretary's voice came through.
"Beauty, look at my phone number, doesn't it feel a little familiar?" Lin Tu hinted, thinking that if the other party said no, he would just say he dialed the wrong number.
But the facts proved that the God-tier Luck effect was still active.
The sweet secretary said in a flustered voice: "You're the Young Master! The President is still in a meeting; otherwise, I can get you..."
"No!"
Lin Tu quickly stopped her. If the real Lin Corporation came, he would definitely be exposed. So Lin Tu thought of a way to extend the effect. He said to the sweet secretary: "I've run into a bit of trouble here at the Heaven and Earth Hotel. Could you find someone with a bit of authority to come and handle it for me?"
"Manager Fang is here; should I ask her to come down and handle it?"
"Yes, her."
Lin Tu hung up the phone.
He noticed that the security guards couldn't help but laugh.
"Hahaha, General Manager Fang? Kid, are you a clown sent by monkeys?"
"Get out of here right now."
"Someone as scruffy as you can call Manager Fang?"
The security guards were enraged.
This hotel was an industry under the Lin Corporation.
And the one in charge of hotel management was Manager Fang, whose slender figure and captivating beauty had long been their goddess.
But just then.
"You all, shut up!"
A stunning beauty, wearing high heels that led up to long, delicate, snow-white legs, walked over. She was dressed in a uniform, which perfectly highlighted her absolutely explosive figure.
She walked with brisk steps.
The sway of her clothes directly made Lin Tu stare in a daze.
Such a top-tier beauty actually worked at a hotel; she should be competing in the Miss World pageant!
"Manager Fang!"
The security guards' eyes were burning with desire; they quickly lowered their heads, then said flatteringly, "Manager Fang, this kid wants to cause trouble here. We're just about to..."
But the security guard hadn't even finished speaking.
Fang Qing had already arrived in front of Lin Tu, bowed, and said, "Young Master, I didn't know you were here for an inspection. Please forgive me."
Lin Tu was dumbfounded.
The moment Fang Qing bowed, he saw a very incredible sight!
And Lin Tu was dumbfounded.
The security guards, on the other hand, were completely stunned!
He really is the Young Master!
