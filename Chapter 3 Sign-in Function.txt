Chapter 3: Check-in Function
Seeing the exquisite beauty before him, respectful and deferential, Lin Tu knew he had succeeded. This beautiful manager must have misunderstood him for the Young Master, following the sweet secretary's instructions.
In other words, even if the time was up later, as long as this misunderstanding wasn't clarified, he would always be the Young Master in Fang Qing's eyes.
“Forget it, I won’t bother with this matter.”
Amidst the security guard's gratitude, Lin Tu walked in.
Fang Qing followed closely behind him.
Although she was very pleasing to the eye, if she came to the private room, she would probably be exposed by Lu Jiawei. So, he could only tell her to go back to work and that he would call her if anything came up.
And because of this, Lin Tu was fortunate enough to get Fang Qing's private contact information.
Arriving at the private room.
Seeing Lin Tu push the door open and enter, the classmates inside were first startled, then Lu Jiawei scoffed, “Oh, Lin Poor, you’re not really going to act like a dog, are you?”
“Young Master Lu, you think too highly of Lin Poor. If he were acting like a dog, would the security guards let such a scruffy animal in?”
“Hahaha.”
Laughter rang out loudly. To curry favor with Lu Jiawei, everyone was very enthusiastic.
Lin Tu ignored them.
He looked around but didn't find his seat.
Just then, Zhang Xueyao couldn't stand it anymore. She stood up and waved to Lin Tu, “Lin Tu, sit next to me.”
At her words, the entire room fell silent again, while Lu Jiawei stared at Lin Tu with a threatening look, as if saying, 'Try sitting over there and see what happens?'
“Alright.”
Lin Tu smiled and sat down next to Zhang Xueyao with great ease.
Before he came in, he had sworn to show Lu Jiawei. Now that the opportunity had arrived, how could he be afraid?
The entire room was silent, waiting for Lu Jiawei to act.
Lin Tu, however, spoke first, “Why are you looking at me? Have you ordered? If not, then I’ll order.”
He was the fake Young Master now; this opportunity shouldn't be wasted.
“Alright, give the menu to Lin Poor!”
Lu Jiawei sneered, handed the menu to Lin Tu, and summoned a waiter.
“Give me everything on this page,” Lin Tu said with a smile, returning the menu to the waiter, who, without thinking, took the menu and was about to leave.
But Lu Jiawei stopped him.
“Wait a moment.”
Then he turned to Lin Tu, “Damn it, Lin Poor, I’m giving you face, aren’t I? Fine, since you ordered these dishes, shouldn’t you be the one to pay the bill? Calculate how much this meal costs for me, and tell him to pay now.”
Lu Jiawei was certain Lin Tu had no money at all.
However, he didn't expect the waiter to explain, “Manager Fang just said that all expenses for your room today are waived.”
“What? My cousin, she…”
Zhang Xueyao almost blurted it out.
No one knew that the famous beautiful manager, Fang Qing, was her cousin.
Her cousin knew she was here to eat, but there was no need to be so polite as to waive the bill. She wasn't the boss; if she waived the bill, wouldn't she be paying out of her own pocket?
After the waiter left, Zhang Xueyao followed him out and called Fang Qing from a corner.
“Zhang Xueyao? What’s up?”
“Cousin, didn’t you know I was at the hotel?”
“You’re at the hotel?”
Hearing her cousin’s surprised voice, Zhang Xueyao immediately understood that her cousin’s waiver was for other people.
So she explained the situation to Fang Qing.
Who knew Fang Qing would say even more surprisingly, “You’re actually the Young Master’s classmate? Why didn’t I hear you mention it?”
“Young Master? I don’t know.”
Zhang Xueyao was confused.
Then, after Fang Qing described the Young Master’s physical features and clothing to Zhang Xueyao, Zhang Xueyao instantly confirmed it.
The Young Master was Lin Tu!
“I knew he was the Young Master! He even lied to me today, saying it was just acting!” Zhang Xueyao was a little exasperated and told Fang Qing everything that happened today.
With these two things said, Lin Tu being the Young Master became even more certain.
“Zhang Xueyao, you must seize this opportunity during this gathering. If you don’t want him, your cousin won’t let him go.”
“Cousin, you know my family’s situation.”
Zhang Xueyao thought of her family’s recent situation and subtly clenched her fist, “I’m just afraid he won’t think highly of me.”
Actually, Zhang Xueyao had planned to borrow money from Lu Jiawei to solve her family’s difficulties when she came this time. But she had just subtly inquired and found that borrowing money was not an issue, but for so much money, she would have to be his Lu family’s daughter-in-law. In other words, she would have to be his girlfriend first to borrow the money.
But the problem was that these were all just Lu Jiawei’s words, and Zhang Xueyao didn’t believe he could make the decision.
But Lin Tu was different.
Five million yuan, to him, probably wasn’t even pocket money.
Soon, the call ended.
After this call, Zhang Xueyao touched up her makeup. Although she really didn't want to do this, for her family, she had to make the sacrifice.
She had also made up her mind.
As long as Lin Tu was willing to lend the money, even if she had to stay at the hotel for one night, she would agree.
Therefore, when Zhang Xueyao returned, she sat next to Lin Tu. By this time, the food and drinks were already served, and she naturally picked up some dishes for Lin Tu.
This scene stunned everyone.
Even Lu Jiawei’s smile froze in place.
“Zhang Xueyao, I only said I’d consider it. You don’t have to use Lin Poor to provoke me, do you?” Lu Jiawei thought Zhang Xueyao’s sudden unusual behavior was because of what he had just said.
“Since Zhang Xueyao is so arrogant, why are you so obsessed with her? Everything she has, I have too. What she won’t do for you, I’ll do for you!”
“Yes, we all can.”
Lin Tu’s mission targets also spoke up.
The implication was obvious.
“Alright.”
Lu Jiawei nodded, “But before that, I have something to settle with Lin Poor. Lobby Manager, bring some security guards in for me. I need to deal with someone. If anything happens, I’ll take responsibility. If he’s beaten to death or crippled, it’s all on me!”
Lu Jiawei had now thrown off all pretense.
So, Zhang Xueyao, you’re so cold, are you? Fine, when your family truly gets into trouble, I'll see if you come knocking on my door, and whether I’ll humiliate you or not!
As for Lin Tu.
He wouldn't leave standing today!
Soon, the lobby manager brought in the security guards.
The classmates wisely moved aside.
“Come, give him a good welcome. Beat him to death. I’ll give you a hundred for every hit.” Lu Jiawei said with a sneer, “Lin Poor, this is the biggest difference between you and me. My family has money, and I can do whatever I want to kill you.”
Lin Tu, however, calmly took a sip of tea.
Seeing that Lu Jiawei had finished his act, he said to the security guards, “Why are you still standing there? Hit him! Are you saying I can’t protect you?”
“Yes, Young Master!”
First, a security guard kicked Lu Jiawei down, and then everyone swarmed him, punching and kicking.
The lobby manager saw this and said indifferently, “Everyone has seen it. It was this Young Master Lu who provoked our Lin Group’s Young Master first. Even if he’s beaten to death, he brought it upon himself.”
At these words.
Everyone in the room looked at Lin Tu in panic.
Especially those female classmates; even their thick makeup couldn't hide their fright.
At the same time.
