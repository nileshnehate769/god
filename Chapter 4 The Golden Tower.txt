Chapter 4: Golden Small Pagoda
Hearing the first half of the system's reward, Lin Tu almost laughed out loud.
Lin's Group was worth trillions, so 10% was 100 billion. He could instantly become the richest man.
But then the 'three-day duration' made him unable to laugh.
What use were shares for three days?
However, Lin Tu still understood the principle of 'don't refuse a freebie'.
"Brother Lin, I was wrong, stop hitting me, I was wrong!"
At this moment, Lu Jiawei was screaming like a pig being slaughtered by the security guards. He truly couldn't understand how Lin Poor had suddenly become the Young Master of the Lin Group.
"Stop hitting him. Just throw him out." Lin Tu waved his hand, and the security guards immediately dragged the unobservant brat out.
The other classmates looked at each other, wanting to speak but not daring to.
This class reunion was destined not to continue.
Lin Tu also didn't want to stay any longer, so he planned to leave, but he didn't expect Zhang Xueyao to chase after him.
"Lin Tu, there's something I want to ask you for help with."
Zhang Xueyao struggled internally for a moment, then pursed her lips and said.
"What's wrong, Senior Sister?"
Seeing Zhang Xueyao's expression, he knew she must have encountered trouble. Zhang Xueyao had come to help him when he had a car accident. If he hadn't stopped her then, Zhang Xueyao would have already compensated the BMW driver on his behalf.
Lin Tu, of course, remembered this kindness.
If he had a chance to repay her, he would go through fire and water.
"It's like this."
Zhang Xueyao explained that her father's company had a debt of five million that hadn't been paid off due to recent poor management. The creditors had recently blocked the company entrance. She wanted to do something for her father, so she wanted to borrow five million from Lin Tu.
After listening, Lin Tu knew that Zhang Xueyao had misunderstood him as the Young Master, and this amount of money was nothing to him.
But he really wasn't.
However, Lin Tu wanted to help with this!
"Senior Sister, can I help you in a different way?"
"Huh?" Zhang Xueyao looked at Lin Tu in confusion.
"What I mean is, if I can bring you a collaboration as a representative of Lin's Group, wouldn't that solve the problem better?"
He had no cash, but Lin Tu had 10% of Lin's Group shares. Although it was only for three days and seemed somewhat useless, now it seemed tailor-made for Zhang Xueyao's situation.
"Are you really willing to help me like this?" Zhang Xueyao was extremely touched. Borrowing money meant repaying it. Even if this crisis was resolved, it only solved the money issue, and the company's main problems were still there. But bringing in a collaboration was different.
Lin's Group.
A trillion-dollar asset company, what other partner could be more stable than this company?
Furthermore.
If her father's business partners knew he had reached a collaboration with Lin's Group, wouldn't they rush to curry favor? Would five million even be an issue then?
"I'll try first. I don't know if it will work." Lin Tu finished speaking and took out his phone to call the sweet secretary.
The divine luck effect had passed, so Lin Tu was no longer the Young Master, but now he was a major Shareholders of Lin's Group.
Hearing a call from a major Shareholders, she naturally responded respectfully.
"This afternoon, a company will come to discuss a collaboration. Their surname is Zhang. His daughter was kind to me, and I want to repay her. Is that okay?"
"If it's a particularly large collaboration, I need to consult the President."
"A collaboration of ten million or so is enough."
"Then there's absolutely no problem."
The sweet secretary recorded this matter.
Such a request from a major Shareholders was not excessive at all.
"It's settled. You can go to Lin's Group this afternoon to discuss the collaboration."
"Lin Tu, thank you so much. I don't even know how to thank you."
Zhang Xueyao's eyes were red with gratitude. She had begged many people, even attending the class reunion and asking Lu Jiawei for help, but she was always met with rejection.
She clearly saw the coldness of human relationships.
But Lin Tu, the dignified Young Master of Lin's Group, helped her without any airs.
She was truly moved by this kindness.
"No need to thank me. Just treat me to a meal sometime in the future to show your appreciation." Lin Tu was eager to find a quiet place to research the system's sign-in function.
"Then let's add contact information first." Zhang Xueyao was worried that Lin Tu might disappear later, so she took out her phone, verified her WeChat addition, and then hurried away, wanting to return to the company to tell her father the news. Although she didn't know if it would succeed, Lin Tu had indeed given her great hope.
What Zhang Xueyao didn't expect was that this collaboration was achieved halfway.
Because Fang Qing had called her.
And also told Zhang Xueyao that the headquarters had assigned her to handle this collaboration and instructed her to satisfy the other party's conditions as much as possible.
While Zhang Xueyao was completely relieved, Lin Tu was already in his hotel room researching the sign-in function.
"Ding-dong"
"Sign-in function activated. Do you want to sign in?"
"Of course, sign in."
As Lin Tu finished speaking, the room suddenly erupted in colorful light, and then a large wheel, about the size of a person, appeared, densely packed with prizes.
Such as Yellow-tier cultivation techniques, basic medical skills, Fifth-tier X-ray vision, 10 billion in cash, and so on.
There were various real-world assets and inheritances, as well as secret arts and martial arts only seen in novels. In short, the items on the wheel were very generous.
However, what Lin Tu found annoying was that half of the wheel's area was 'Thank you for participating'!
"Sign-in system once a month"
"Items obtained from each sign-in will be directly infused into the host's real world."
"Start drawing?"
Hearing the system's prompt, Lin Tu hesitated.
He considered himself to have particularly bad luck. He had only had a few lucky breaks since childhood. If he were to draw directly, Lin Tu felt that he would 90% draw 'Thank you for participating'.
Just then.
Lin Tu suddenly remembered that he still had his third divine luck!
"System, can I use divine luck for the draw!"
Lin Tu waited expectantly for the system's reply.
If the system said no, then he had nothing to say. He could only rely on luck to draw, and if he drew 'Thank you for participating', he would just pretend nothing happened.
"Do you want to use Divine Luck to empower the draw? The effect is only once."
"Yes!"
Without even thinking, Lin Tu directly said yes.
Then the wheel began to spin.
As the wheel spun, Lin Tu's heart was also filled with anticipation, hoping that even if it wasn't 10 billion in cash, he could at least get a world-class villa.
And in Lin Tu's anticipation, the wheel's speed slowed down more and more.
Finally, the pointer landed on an area only a millimeter in size.
Then the wheel emitted a colorful glow.
A cultivation technique.
A piece of armor.
A long spear.
A small pouch.
And a golden small pagoda.
"Congratulations to the host for extremely fortunately obtaining the ultimate reward: Grand Prize of the Heavens"
